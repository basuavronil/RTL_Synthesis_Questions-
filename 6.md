## 🔄 Classic Interview Question: Swapping Two Registers in One Clock Cycle

Swapping the values of two registers in a single clock cycle is a classic digital design interview question. It highlights the fundamental conflict between **parallel hardware reality** and **sequential software execution**.

Here is a detailed breakdown of how and why swapping works (or fails) depending on whether you use non-blocking or blocking assignments.

---

### 1. The Ideal Way: Using Non-Blocking Assignments (`<=`)

If you want to swap two registers (`a` and `b`) so that they exchange values on the clock edge, non-blocking assignments achieve this naturally and cleanly:

```verilog
always @(posedge clk) begin
    a <= b;
    b <= a;
end
