### FAQ: What logic does an `if-else if` chain make vs. a `case` statement?

* **`if-else if` Chain:** Implements **priority logic** (a cascade or chain of multiplexers). Conditions are evaluated sequentially from top to bottom, meaning the first true condition takes precedence. 
  * *Hardware Impact:* Creates a deeper combinational logic path, leading to higher propagation delay as the number of branches grows.
* **`case` Statement (Fully Specified):** Implements **parallel logic** (typically a single-tier multiplexer or parallel decoder structure). All branch comparisons happen concurrently.
  * *Hardware Impact:* Results in more balanced arrival times, lower combinational path delay, and efficient resource utilization when fully specified.
